Best yoga straps for 2026

Quick answer

The Manduka Align Yoga Strap is our top pick for its sturdy cotton weave, smooth buckle, and comfortable grip.

The Gaiam Yoga Strap is a close runner-up for budget shoppers, while the Hugger Mugger 8-Foot suits tall practitioners, the Nayoya 6-Foot is the best value, and the Manduka Unfold 10-Foot is the premium choice for deep stretching.

A yoga strap might look like a simple piece of fabric, but it’s one of the most versatile props you can own. It extends your reach in poses like seated forward folds, helps you maintain alignment in binds and shoulder openers, and deepens stretches without forcing your body past its limits. Whether you’re a beginner working on hamstring flexibility or an experienced yogi refining your alignment, a strap is a small investment that pays off in every practice. When choosing a strap, think about length, material, and buckle quality. Most straps run 6 to 10 feet; longer ones are better for taller bodies or for poses that require extra slack. Cotton webbing is durable and breathable, while nylon or polyester feels smoother and may slide less. The buckle should move easily but hold firmly when you need it. A good strap is one you forget you’re using, until you notice how much deeper you can stretch.
